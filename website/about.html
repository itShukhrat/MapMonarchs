About

A board laid over the real world.

MapMonarchs is a territorial strategy game played on foot. The map of Uzbekistan is divided into hexagonal cells; you claim the one you stand in, and hold your ground against everyone else.

What it is

A premium, honest, competitive game about presence. Territory is decided on our servers from the route you actually walk — which is what keeps it fair. Your identity is a callsign and a colour, derived from your account, that appear on the map as the ground you hold.

What it is not

  • Not a navigation or travel app.
  • Not a tracker — it never follows you in the background.
  • Not pay-to-win — money will never buy territorial power.

Where it is

MapMonarchs is on Android via Google Play, package com.mapmonarchs. The playable world is Uzbekistan.
