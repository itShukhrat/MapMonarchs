Safety & Community

MapMonarchs sends real people walking in the real world and is building toward social features. These rules keep it trustworthy.

Play safely

  • Stay aware of your surroundings. Do not play while driving.
  • Obey local laws and never enter unsafe, private, or restricted places to claim ground.
  • The game rewards walking, not risk. No cell is worth your safety.

Keep it fair

  • No cheating. Location spoofing and movement automation are treated as abuse and can cost you territory or access.
  • No impersonation. Callsigns are derived from your account and cannot be forged; do not attempt to pose as others.
  • No harassment. Treat other players with respect.

Player-visible content

Not yet enabled Public profiles, stories, and other player-visible content are not active. Before any such feature turns on, reporting, blocking, and content review will be in place, and you will be asked to accept the applicable rules before posting.

To report a safety or abuse concern, contact support@mapmonarchs.com.
