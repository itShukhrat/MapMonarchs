Privacy Policy

What MapMonarchs collects, why, who processes it, how long it stays, and how to remove it. This reflects the app as built — where a library has a capability we do not use, it is stated as off.

Applies to  com.mapmonarchsVersion  1.0Updated  11 August 2026

Who we are

MapMonarchs is operated by Shukhrat Elmurodov (“we”). You sign in with an emailed code — there is no password. While the map is open, the app reads your precise location so our servers can decide whether you may claim the cell you are standing in. Ownership is decided server-side, never by your device.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — authentication, database, realtime, and server functions. Holds your email, account, location log, and game state. Acts as our processor.
  • Resend — delivers your sign-in code email; receives your email address for that purpose.
  • Mapbox — renders the map; receives the map view (derived from your location) to fetch the correct tiles.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash diagnostics, only if you opt in.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. These providers process data on our behalf to run the game; they are not given your data for their own purposes.

Location

MapMonarchs uses precise, foreground location — only while the app is open and the map is on screen. There is no background tracking, no location service that runs when the app is closed, and no record of where you have been beyond the 30-day anti-cheat log above. Claims are only allowed on a fix accurate to within 30 metres. Other players can see the ground you hold — never your position.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and personal data at any time, in the app or by request — see Delete your account.

One accurate detail Territory is shared game-world state. When you delete your account, the ground you held is not erased; its owner is set to none, so the cells become neutral and claimable by others. Nothing about the vacated ground identifies you.

Security & your rights

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Game data is governed server-side: your device cannot assert ownership, entitlement, rank, or another player’s identity. To ask about your data or exercise your rights, contact support@mapmonarchs.com.
