Command is the quiet layer over the map: who you are, the ground you hold, and the moves that are actually open to you. It doesn't invent numbers — it shows you the state of your game.

01 · Who you are

A crest and a colour,
read at a glance.

Your callsign and colour sit at the top of command, the same identity that marks your ground on the map. No profile to curate — just the sovereign the board recognises.

02 · What you hold

Your ground, as it stands.

The shape you've built, drawn from the server's own record so it agrees with what everyone else sees. When a cell changes hands — yours or a rival's — command reflects it, because the map already did.

03 · What you can do

that are real.

Command shows what's live today, and marks what's on the way — never dressing a plan up as a feature.
