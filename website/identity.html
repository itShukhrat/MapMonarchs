Identity

A name you didn't choose,
and can't lose.

You don't build a profile in MapMonarchs. Your callsign and your colour are computed from your account — the same on every device, after every reinstall, and impossible for anyone else to wear.

01 · The callsign

Given, not typed.

There's no username field, because a name you pick is a name someone else can fake. Your callsign is derived from your account and shown on the ground you hold — so a crest on the map is always the player it claims to be.

02 · The colour

One colour is yours.
Your ground wears it.

Every player gets a colour, fixed to their identity. It's how you read the board at a glance: your territory glows in it, rivals in theirs. Nobody can copy your colour any more than they can copy your ground.

03 · The presence

Identity isn't a profile.
It's where you've stood.

The board doesn't know you by a bio or an avatar you uploaded. It knows you by the cells you've walked into and kept. Your reputation is a shape on a map, earned on foot.
