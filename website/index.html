Territory Strategy

Ground you walk becomes ground you hold.

MapMonarchs turns the real map of Uzbekistan into a board. Walk into a hexagonal cell, claim it, and hold it against everyone else. Territory is decided on our servers, from where you actually go — which is what keeps it fair.

How it plays

  • Sign in with a code. No password — we email you a six-digit code.
  • Claim where you stand. With the map open, a good GPS fix lets you claim the cell you are in.
  • Hold the board. Your ground is drawn in your colour; rivals can take it by walking there themselves.

MapMonarchs is a strategy game, not a navigation app. It never tracks you in the background, and other players see the ground you hold — never where you are.
