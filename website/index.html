01 · The hex

One hexagon.
The atom of territory.

Every place on Earth sits inside exactly one map cell — a hexagon roughly a football pitch across. The whole game is built on them: you never own a point or a pin, you own a cell. Which cell you're in is derived from your GPS position on our servers, so it can't be spoofed by naming a hex the client didn't earn.

One cell. One piece of ground.

02 · The claim

You don't tap to claim.
You stand there.

A claim needs a real GPS fix, accurate to within 30 metres. The app sends your raw coordinates — never a hex you picked — and the server decides the cell, checks the fix is plausible, and writes ownership. Presence is the whole cost. That's what keeps the board honest.

Fix ≤ 30 m→ Server validates→ Hex secured
03 · The territory

Ground accumulates.
Borders appear.

Held cells fill with your colour and knit into a shape that is unmistakably yours. Your territory is drawn from authoritative server geometry — the same ground, seen the same way by everyone on the map.

Your ground A rival's Unclaimed
04 · The contest

Nothing you hold
is permanent.

Another player can walk into your cell and take it — the same way you took it. No cell is safe because you paid; it's safe because you keep showing up. When ownership changes, the map updates for everyone in real time.

05 · The command

A callsign.
A colour. A board.

Your identity is a sovereign callsign and a territory colour, derived from your account so no one can impersonate it. From the command view you read your standing on the world you're building.

Location, handled honestly

A territory game can respect
where you are.

Location is the whole mechanic, so we take the least of it that works.

  • Precise location only while the app is open — never in the background.
  • Other players see the ground you hold, never where you are or where you've been.
  • The anti-cheat location log is kept 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Delete your account in the app — your data goes; your ground is released back to the map.
