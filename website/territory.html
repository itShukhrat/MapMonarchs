01 · The border

A border is only
held edges.

There's no wall to build and no line to draw. Your border is simply the outer cells you're still holding — the bright rim of your ground. Lose an edge cell and the border moves in; take one and it moves out.

02 · The pressure

Rivals push
where you're thin.

Another player walks into an edge cell and it turns. Not because they bought it — because they were standing there and you weren't. A thin border is an invitation; a deep one is hard to move.

03 · The answer

You take it back
the only way.

By being there again. Ground changes hands through presence, both directions — so a contested edge isn't a loss, it's a reason to walk back out. When it flips, the whole map sees it, live.
